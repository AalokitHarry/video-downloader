How it works

  1. Copy the link. Grab the URL of any video, Reel, pin, or post from TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest, Reddit, X, or Facebook.
  2. Paste it and pick a format. Drop the link into the box above and choose Video, MP3, or Photo.
  3. Download. The highest-quality file is fetched straight to your device — no account, no browser extension, no ad-gated "download" buttons.

Supported platforms

This tool runs on yt-dlp, so it works with TikTok, Instagram (including Reels), Pinterest, Reddit, X/Twitter, Facebook, and hundreds of other sites. TikTok and Reddit download reliably without logging in. Instagram and X increasingly require a login just to view video on their own site — when that happens here, it's the platform's restriction, not a bug. Photo posts are supported too: Instagram and X carousels come back as every photo in the post, Pinterest pins as the original-resolution image, and Reddit and Facebook as their preview image. YouTube isn't supported: it reliably blocks this kind of hosted tool at the network level, independent of any fix on our end.

Frequently asked questions

Is this video downloader free?

Yes — no account, subscription, or payment is required for video, MP3, or photo downloads.

Do I need to install an app or browser extension?

No. Everything runs in your browser — paste a link and the file downloads straight to your device.

Will the downloaded video have a watermark?

No. You get the original, unmodified file the platform serves — this tool doesn't add or remove watermarks.

Can I download Instagram Reels or TikTok videos?

Yes — both are supported the same way as regular videos. Paste the Reel or TikTok link and choose Video.

Why did my download fail?

Usually because the video is private, age-restricted, or the platform is requiring a login just to view it — common on Instagram and X. This tool only fetches publicly accessible media.

Is it legal to download videos this way?

Downloading itself isn't illegal, but what you do with the file is subject to the same copyright rules as anywhere else. See the Terms & Disclaimer page for details.
