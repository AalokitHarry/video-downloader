Privacy Policy

What this site does with data

Short version: the tool itself keeps nothing. The ads on this page, if any are showing, work the way ads normally do.

What the download tool collects

Nothing persistent. There's no account, no login, and no cookies set by this tool. The link you paste is used once, to fetch your file, and isn't logged or stored anywhere. The file itself is deleted from the server the moment it finishes sending to your device — nothing you download sits on this server afterward.

Standard server logs

Like effectively every website, basic technical information (your IP address, the time of your request, which page or endpoint you hit) passes through the server as part of normal operation. This isn't written to a database or used for tracking — it's the same baseline logging any web server does, and it isn't tied to anything you download.

Advertising

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google, not this site, sets its own cookies and collects data (such as your IP address, device information, and ad interaction) to serve and measure those ads — that happens under Google's own privacy practices, not this site's. You can see what Google collects and adjust ad personalization at myadcenter.google.com, and read Google's own policy at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

No selling, no sharing

This site doesn't sell data, because it doesn't collect any to sell. It doesn't share anything with third parties beyond the ad serving described above.
